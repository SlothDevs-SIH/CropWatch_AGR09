# The known event: Maharashtra drought, 2026

What we could confirm from public reporting (3 November 2026), and what we could not.

## Confirmed
- The state government declared drought in **265 of 358 talukas** by a Government Resolution dated **25 September 2026** (Relief and Rehabilitation Department). Reports: [Down To Earth](https://www.downtoearth.org.in/natural-disasters/maharashtra-declares-drought-in-265-talukas-as-crop-losses-mount-after-weak-monsoon), [Daily Pioneer](https://dailypioneer.com/news/maharashtra-declares-265-talukas-drought-affected-announces-relief), [Deccan Chronicle](https://www.deccanchronicle.com/nation/drought-declared-in-265-talukas-across-maharashtra-1990850).
- The total was later raised to **305** talukas after a review (reported mid-October 2026). [The CSR Journal](https://thecsrjournal.in/maharashtra-government-declares-305-talukas-drought-affected-announces-financial-aid-and-relief-measures-for-farmers-amid-severe-water-scarcity)
- Jalna district is named among the affected districts, with a reported rainfall deficit of about **46%** (one report; not cross-checked against the India Meteorological Department).

## Our own independent check (rainfall)
NASA POWER daily rainfall (coarse grid, about 50 km), 1 June to 31 October:

| Box | 2026 | Average 2018 to 2025 | 2026 as share of average |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jalna | 423 mm | about 814 mm | about 52% |
| Partur | 439 mm | about 800 mm | about 55% |

2026 is the **driest of the nine seasons** since 2018 at both boxes. This agrees in direction and size with the reported deficit. It is a coarse estimate, and Partur and Ghansawangi fall in the same grid cell.

## Not confirmed (blocking the taluka-level check)
- **Which of Jalna's eight talukas are on the 265 list or the added 40.** No source found gives the taluka names for Jalna. The Government Resolution annex or the Jalna collectorate notices are needed. Until then, the check against "declared or not" cannot be done per taluka, and we do not claim it.
- **Crop-cutting experiment results** and insurance payouts for the season: not available yet.

## What this means for the pitch
- The **order date (25 September 2026)** is solid and is what "days of warning" is measured against.
- The **drought itself** is supported by reporting and by our rainfall check.
- We can **not** say any particular Jalna taluka was officially declared until the annex is read.
